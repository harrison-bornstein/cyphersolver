### Summaries (English; L2-L5 and L6 ll. 1-3 from the gloss, not from the signs)

- **L1** (signs): the Sultan's camp sends no news; perhaps, having nothing worth reporting, he sends nothing
  and lets no one else send any; news of some important event is expected from day to day.
- **L2** (gloss): Aramon (French envoy) wrote to the King of France; Cambrai opened and read the letters.
  Aramon says the King should not be surprised if his embassy costs more than before; dearth is great in
  the Sultan's army; all will go as the King wishes; when he left Constantinople there were armed ships
  with Dragut and twenty galleys with Salih Reis, the rest of the fleet being caulked and readied for any
  French request; if the Venetian bailo had not worked against France, things would go better this year
  and the Sultan would not be going into Persia.
- **L3** (gloss): the King should take heart, for next year great preparations will be made to ruin both
  Majesties (Charles V and Ferdinand); Aramon wrote much more of the kind to the King of France.
- **L4** (gloss): Malvezzi has both reports from a reliable source; they contradict each other: to Cambrai
  Aramon says all goes well in the army (to spread good news here), to the King privately that dearth is great.
- **L5** (gloss): Ibrahim Pasha and Yunus Bey said it is a disgrace that these Frenchmen come with so many
  lies about the Sultan, and asked what importance Captain Bartolomeo's business can have, he having been
  here a month and now in such a hurry to leave.
- **L6** (ll. 1-3 gloss, ll. 4-5 signs): Fumel gave the Pasha a Turkish copy of letters he says came lately
  from the King of France; Malvezzi encloses a copy, *and also sends Your Majesty copies of two letters of
  Frater Georg recently addressed to the Pasha here in Constantinople* — i.e. enclosures B and C.
- **B** (signs, all 15 lines, pass 3): a Frater Georg letter to Ibrahim Pasha dated Corde, 28 July 1548, not either 7-4-5 exemplar; see "Copy B".
- **C** (signs, ll. 1-8): *exemplum literarum ... [Illus]trissime Domine, domine michi [obseruandissime?] ...
  seruitiorum meorum comendationem ... potentissimus et clementissimus [Imperator] ... [in] omnibus
  necessitatibus meis et in ... [Dominationem] uestram Illustrissimam semper ... aut per ... potentissimi
  Caesaris ...* A Frater Georg letter (heading + address + commendation of service). It is **not identical
  to exemplar C of 7-4-5**: the cipher has *domine michi* (exemplar: *magnifice Domine, domine
  obseruandissime*), *seruitiorum meorum*, and phrases not in exemplar C (*potentissimus et clementissimus*,
  *in omnibus necessitatibus meis*); at ~30 lines it is also about three times as long as exemplar C. Its
  wording sits closer to exemplar B (*Mandauerat potentissimus Turcarum Imperator ...*), but it is not
  exemplar B either. Identity open until ll. 9-30 are read.

## Sign variants found in this hand (7-4-9)

- **cup with a taller left upright = g** (*degne*); **small round cup / open arch = l** — confirmed.
- **o** has four forms: **°P**, **∂**, **looped g (§)** (*non*, *auisino*, *aspettano*, *mando*, *copia*),
  and the stacked circle-on-stem (*forsi*, *doi*). In R366 the looped g was read as *d*; not here.
- **d**: dotted circle **⊙** (*di*, *de*, *doi*) and the **8-shape** (*degne*, *dare*, *Domine*); the
  stacked circle-on-stem also serves for d (*dirette*, *comendationem*). The same 8-shape is **q** in *qua*.
- **circle-on-stem with a second small circle at the foot** is the busiest sign: e (*per*, *che*, *lettere*),
  o (*forsi*), d (*de*, *dirette*), r (*presente*), and with a cross t. Resolved by context; marked ? where open.
- **ʛ (g with a tail) = r**, sometimes i (*doi*, *comendationem*, *potentissimus*) — a mild variant.
- **#o = e**; **π / Π = s**; **Δ / ∇ = et**; **hooked b = n**; **7 = ll** and also **i**; **12 = tt, 13 = ss**.
- Nulls: *etiam, spo, spu, Porro, Cum, atq, Dar, Der, Idcirco*, loose *b, d, n, z, π*.
- Spelling: *cessaris* for *Caesaris* (C l. 8, marked {Caesaris}); *michi* for *mihi* (C l. 2).

## Copy B (pass 3, 31 Oct 2026)

Signs: `749_B_signs.txt` (15 lines, not ~14). Reading: line `749B` of `749_reading.txt`. Single reader, from the
1565-px screenshots stitched into one page (crops at 2.6-3x).

**Identity: it is NOT exemplar B (6 June) or exemplar C (2 July) of 7-4-5.** It is a third letter of Frater
Georg to Ibrahim Pasha. From the signs, its close reads *corde uigesima octaua iulii millesimo quingentesimo
quadragesimo octauo et loco suo seruitor <zos> episcopus uaradiensis*, i.e. **dated "Corde", 28 July 1548**
(the same place name as 745B's *datum corde decuria*). None of its readable sentences match either exemplar
word for word. The "B." in the margin (beside l. 8) is Malvezzi's enclosure letter, not the 7-4-5 lettering.
This matches the L6 statement that he encloses two *recent* ("nouamente") letters of Frater Georg. Copy C
should be checked against this finding too: it also does not match its exemplar.

What the readable parts say (heading and address, then): *exemplum literarum [torn] Illustrissime domine
obse[ruandissime, torn] seruitiorum meorum comendationem ... <bellum> ... [h]abebam ad maiestatem sultan
Soleiman ... illorum quid ... <bellum> ... esse ... homines ... Ferdinandus ... aliud bellum futurum si ... uolui
dominationi uestrae illustrissimae et maiestati suae ... dare. Nichilominus litteras ... dominationi uestrae
illustrissimae. Rogo ut maiestate sua ordinare uelit, ut si necesse fuerit in promptu habeamus.* Then the date
and signature. In short: he wrote earlier to the Sultan about Ferdinand and a war to come, and asks Ibrahim to
have the Sultan order that help be at hand when needed. The request parallels exemplar C's, but the wording is new.

Slip-corrected words `{..}` (13): *seruitiorum* (r of ser- not written), *ferdinandus* (no r; a z null in its
place), *illustrissimae* x2 (signs give illustr..sima/-e with one letter short), *dominationi* (last sign
unclear), *maiestate* (a doubled), *necesse* (one extra sign), *habeamus* (second h-like sign blotted),
*uigesima* (Π doubled), *iulii* (one unclear stroke), *millesimo* (two unclear signs), *episcopus*,
*uaradiensis* (one sign each doubtful). *sultan* and *soleiman* are read: every sign is clear (Π‡ = s, ⊃ = l).
