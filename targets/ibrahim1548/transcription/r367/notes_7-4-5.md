# HHStA StAbt Türkei I 7-4-5 (f. 31-34): Frater Georg to Ibrahim Pasha, 1548, deciphered with R367

Work of 30-31 Oct 2026, from the AIS screenshots in `ais/745_*`. These copies were deciphered with a known key
(R367). The Latin exemplars on f. 34 were used only as a check: a value that only the exemplar supports is marked
`{..}` or `[..]`, never written as plain text. Single reader, no second check.

## Summaries

**Letter B (Frater Georg to Ibrahim Pasha, "Corde de curia"(?), 6 June 1548).** The Sultan ordered him to report
anything certain he learned, so he has written to the Emperor about some news. He asks Ibrahim to forward his letters
to the Sultan. The force (*bellum*) King Ferdinand has sent into Hungary is sent only against the Hungarians who
disobeyed him and is no threat to the Sultan; if it were, he would long ago have written to the Sultan and to Ibrahim.
He lives under the Sultan's protection in his part of Hungary. They have no good wheat because rain spoiled it in the
fields. Closing, date and signature: Frater Georgius, bishop of Várad.

**Letter C (same to same, "in arce Viiuar", 2 July 1548).** Various rumours reach them, and they fear their enemies
will attack while the Sultan is away. He asks Ibrahim to order Chazum (Kasım) Pasha, the Sultan's lieutenant at Buda and in
Hungary, and Mehmed Pasha to send them help when needed and requested. After God they have no refuge but the Sultan.
Closing, date and signature as above.

## Key and sign variants (this hand)

The R367 values hold throughout. Variants and corrections:
- The **9 with a crossed tail = r** and the **9 with a smooth tail = x** (*rex*, *exemplum*, *auxilio*, *sexta*). This
  confirms the f. 29 transcribers' correction: the KEY_BRIEF has these two reversed.
- **h-hook = n** everywhere; **λ = h** (*hic*, *habemus*, *hongaros*).
- A looped g (ϑd) = d; a footed circle (σ, ϑq) = q (*quid*, *quoniam*, *quae*); P with a circle below = o; the plain
  Π and Ш (Π with a base bar) = g (*igitur*, *magnifice*, *dignetur*, *regno*); Π with a right-leg tick = s.
- Small round cup, ∪/∩ and the open arch = l.
- The circle-on-stem (ϙ) is u in most words and e/ae in some (*uelit*, *debeant*); I resolved it word by word, and
  the reading stays the same either way.
- **New sign: Ш with three bars + o = annus**, used in 745C *die iulii <anno> millesimo*. This matches the key's "annus" sign.
- 745C (img2) writes x in *maximum* with a triple-barred cross. This sign is not in the key, so the word is left
  `[ma?imum]`.
- *Chazum* (745C, 745X3): the z is written as a three-humped m-like sign that is not in the key, since R367 has no z.
  Left as `[cha?um]` and `[ca?um]`.
- Code words are confirmed: zos = Frater Georgius (always `zos.` between full stops), Ka = Passa (also Bassae),
  Ras = Turcarum Imperator, Sis = Ibrahim Passa (745X3 *ad Sis* for *ad Hebrai Bassam*), zi = Hungaria, 50 = bellum.
  The same word is spelled out in one copy and coded in its twin: 745B writes out *bellum* and *hongariam*, where 745X4
  has 50 and zi; 745X3 writes out *hongariae* and *anno*, where 745C has zi and the annus sign.
- Nulls: porro, etiam, idcirco, cum, atq, spo, spu, Der, Dar, and the loose b d n z π. 745B L01 also ends with
  Latin-letter "et" after "zos", which I took as a null. In 745B, π and n are also written *inside* words
  (*exemplum*, *illustrissime*), so loose nulls can be inserted within a word.
- 745C L01 has *aliud* written above the line with an insertion mark, enclosed by the nulls b n ... π.

## Disagreements with the exemplars and between copies

Letter B (745B vs 745X4 vs exemplar B):
- Opening: 745X4 begins *replica exemplum ...*, and so does 745X3 (cf. f. 29 *replica*); 745B has no *replica*.
  Neither copy has the exemplar's "Ill.me D.ne &c." alone: 745X4 enciphers the full *illustrissime domine
  obseruandissime seruitiorum meorum comendationem*; 745B has *illustrissime domine et (= &c.)*.
- *potentissimus <Ras>* (745B) against *potentissimus imperator* (745X4, without Turcarum). The exemplar has
  *potentissimus Turcarum Imperator*.
- 745X4 adds `[enus]` after *igitur* (signs e/u-n-u-s; not in the exemplar, unread).
- *mittere* (745B, as in the exemplar) against *transmittere* (745X4).
- 745B *iussit* where the exemplar and 745X4 have *misit* (*contra Hungaros tantum misit*). Every sign is clear.
- 745B has `[es]` (K a) after *inobedientes*. It is not in the exemplar, and K a could also be the code *Ka*, but
  "Passa" makes no sense there.
- *si quid eius [dem] esset* in both copies: the exemplar is torn after *eius de...*. Both copies give *dem* (⊙ K/#o f).
- 745X4 adds *hac* after *dudum* (λ = +; the signs are clear, but the word is not in the exemplar).
- Both copies read *corrupuerunt* (exemplar *corruperunt*): the signs are clear, so this is probably the spelling of
  the source of both copies.
- The place in the date is *corde decuria* in both copies. This confirms my uncertain reading of the exemplar,
  "Corde de curia".
- 745B *sexta Iunii* (as in the exemplar), 745X4 *sesto* (slip, {sexto}). 745B has *et* written in Latin-letter-like signs
  (ϙ̊ ♀) before *loco suo*.
- Signature: 745B writes out *frater georgius*; 745X4 uses *zos*.
- The cipher copies fill the gaps where the right edge of f. 34v is torn: *de[m]*, *Ill[ustrissimae]*,
  *in [parte]*, *propte[r]*, *Dominationem v[estram]*.

Letter C (745C vs 745X3 vs exemplar C):
- Both copies open with *ac magnifice* (the exemplar has *Illustrissime magnifice*), and both spell *comendationem*,
  *suplico*, *necesitas* and *absessum* (exemplar *abscessum*).
- *aduersarii nostri* (745C, as in the exemplar) against *aduersarii uestri* (745X3).
- *item domino Mehmeth Bassae*: 745X3 *mehemet <Ke>*; in 745C the name is lost at the torn edge (`[eu..]` then *a.*).
- *per nosque* (745C "per nos que") against *per nos* (745X3); 745X3 adds *nobis* before *esse* (*auxilio nobis esse*).
- Date: 745C omits *quadragesimo*. The scribe began *qua...*, struck it, wrote *ingentesi* above the line and
  continued *-mo octauo*, so this copy is dated "millesimo quingentesimo octauo". 745X3 has the full year.
- *secondo* (745C signs) for *secundo*; *uiiuar* in both copies for the exemplar's *Vijuar* (Vinea).
- Closing: 745X3 omits *eiusdem ... seruitor* and reads *<zos> uaradiensis [?] episcopus*; 745C follows the exemplar.
- Headings: 745X3 *replica exemplum literarum zos ad Sis*; 745C *aliud exemplum literarum zos ad [he..is] Ka*. The
  exemplar's heading *Aliud exemplum literarum fratris Georgij ad Hebraj Bassam* is itself enciphered in both
  (with *replica* in X3).

## Unread spots, with reasons

745B (3): `[es]` L10, signs give *es*, not in the exemplar (could be Ka, which makes no sense); `[dem]` L13, signs
give *dem*, no confirming text because the exemplar is torn; `[dom?s]` L22, the abbreviated *Dominationis*, two
middle signs unclear (a reversed c and a hook), so I could not tell how the abbreviation was written.
745X4 (3): `[qui?]` L03, last sign of *quid* unclear (π-like); `[enus]` L04, clear signs giving no word in the
exemplar; `[dem]` L10, as in 745B.
745C (5): `[he..is]` L01-02 and `[eu..]` L09 (Mehmeth) are cut off at the torn right edge; `[uaradien..]` L21 is
cut off; `[cha?um]` L08 has an unknown z sign; `[ma?imum]` L14 has an unknown x sign (triple-barred cross).
745X3 (2): `[ca?um]` L06 has an unknown sign (z); `[?]` L16 is one loose Latin-like *e* between nulls, perhaps a null.

Slip-corrected `{..}` words (each one is a missing, doubled or wrong sign, and its signs are in the `_signs` file):
745B 9, 745X4 12, 745C 5, 745X3 9. Most are a dropped letter (*perscrberem*, *maestatem*, *ilustrisimam*,
*abscessum*) or a doubled one (*illusstrisimam*).

## Caveats

- One reader, working from 1565-px-wide screenshots. Doubtful signs carry `?` in the `_signs` files. The per-sign
  transcription is by word (sign groups) rather than a fixed-label sign list as in `f29_lines_*`, and the sign
  labels are glyph shapes, so a sign count from these files is approximate.
- The exemplar was open during transcription. The ambiguous circle-on-stem family (u/e/ae) was resolved by Latin
  context, which the key brief allows; no other value was taken from the exemplar without marking it.
- Exemplar B on f. 34v is torn at the right edge, and I read the place name "Corde de curia" with doubt. Exemplar C
  agrees with the maintainer's `texts/exemplar_7-4-5_C.txt` (minor spelling points in `745_latin.txt`).
- Possible memory items for the main session: none (no user facts learned).
